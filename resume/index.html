How I work

The work isn’t the interface. It’s the problem underneath it, and the people who’ll live with whatever gets built. I’m brought in when the change is genuinely hard to land: legacy environments, regulated settings, classified programs, non-negotiable timelines.

My approach: resolve the logic before the pixels, treat the constraints as the brief, and hand the pen to the people who’ll resist the change. When the most resistant person in the room helps build the new system, they stop defending the old one.
