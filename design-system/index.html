Design system

The system behind adamatteia.com

One accent does all the work. The site is light by default and monochromatic — one hue carries every heading, every line of body text, the links, and the fixed frame; grey is allowed only for small metadata. That restraint is the design, not a shortage of ideas: hierarchy comes from size, weight, and space, never a second colour. Underneath it’s Atteia 1.0 tokens, exposed through Tailwind’s @theme inline bridge and set in Plus Jakarta Sans, with Geist Mono for labels.

Switch the palette and every token, swatch, and component below recolours at once — that’s the system working, not a screenshot of one. Use Jump to section in the header to move around.

01

Brand & approach

One accent, a white page, and a fixed cobalt frame — the approach is modelled on designersinstitute.nz. The only supporting voice is ink: the accent's own darker shadow, kept for structural rules and quiet emphasis. Nothing on the page is there to decorate.

02

Colour

Three things to look at: the semantic tokens that name every colour role, the four palettes they resolve to, and the neutral scale underneath them all. There's still no rainbow — one hue does the work, with grey held in reserve for metadata and surfaces. The site touches only a few neutral steps; the full ramp is the considered set they're drawn from, and worth showing whole.

Semantic tokens

Each one paints with var(--token), so it recolours the instant you switch palette in the header — live, not a fixed set of hexes.

Typography

Two typefaces, and the split of work is strict. Plus Jakarta Sans — a geometric grotesque, weights 300–800 — does display, body, and titles. Geist Mono is held back for labels, numbering, and domain tags. The hierarchy is earned with size and weight, not a third family.

Motion

One easing curve carries everything, and all of it is gated behind prefers-reduced-motion: with reduced motion, no JS, or a failed bundle, content renders fully visible at rest.

Easing
cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1) — every reveal and the palette wash share one curve.
Scroll reveal
Rows rise 12px and fade as they enter the viewport (data-reveal="rise").
Hairline draw
Rules scale in from the left as their row appears (data-reveal="rule").
Hero entrance
The opening headline settles in word by word, ~34ms apart.
Testimonial
Quotes crossfade up as each takes the stage in the carousel.
Palette swap
A circular clip-path reveal expands from the chosen swatch (View Transitions API).
The easing, liveHover the marker — it travels on the shared curve.
